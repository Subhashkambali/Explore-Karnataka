Discover Mysuru

Explore the royal heritage, architecture, traditions and attractions of Mysuru.

THE CITY OF PALACES

Mysuru

Mysuru, also known as Mysore, is one of the most well-known cultural destinations in Karnataka. The city is famous for its royal heritage, magnificent architecture, traditional celebrations and beautiful surroundings.

Mysore Palace

The magnificent Mysore Palace is one of the most recognizable landmarks of the city. Its grand architecture, decorated interiors and royal history make it an important attraction for visitors.

Places to Visit

  • Mysore Palace
  • Chamundi Hill
  • Brindavan Gardens
  • Mysuru Zoo
  • St. Philomena's Church

Culture & Food

Mysuru is also associated with traditional handicrafts, sandalwood products, Mysore silk and the famous Mysore Pak sweet.

VIJAYANAGARA HERITAGE

Hampi

Hampi is a historic destination famous for the remarkable ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire. The area contains temples, monuments, stone structures and dramatic landscapes.

Historical Importance

Hampi preserves the architectural legacy of one of South India's important historical kingdoms. Its temples, markets and monuments provide a glimpse into the region's rich past.

Places to Visit

  • Virupaksha Temple
  • Vittala Temple
  • Stone Chariot
  • Hampi Bazaar
  • Matanga Hill

Experience Hampi

Visitors can explore ancient monuments, temples, stone pathways and scenic viewpoints while experiencing the historic atmosphere of this remarkable destination.

LAND OF COFFEE

Discover Coorg

Explore green hills, coffee plantations, forests and peaceful landscapes.

KODAGU

Coorg

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is a popular hill destination known for its green landscapes, coffee plantations, forests and pleasant surroundings.

Natural Beauty

The region is surrounded by hills, forests and plantations. Its scenic landscapes make it a popular destination for people looking for nature and peaceful surroundings.

Places to Visit

  • Abbey Falls
  • Raja's Seat
  • Mandalpatti
  • Dubare Elephant Camp
  • Namdroling Monastery

Famous For

Coorg is especially known for coffee plantations, beautiful hills, waterfalls and its distinctive local culture.

Discover Gokarna

BEACHES & TEMPLES

Gokarna

Gokarna is a coastal destination known for its beaches, temples and relaxed atmosphere. It combines natural beauty with cultural and spiritual attractions.

Beaches

The area is known for scenic beaches surrounded by hills and coastal landscapes. Visitors can enjoy peaceful walks and beautiful views along the coast.

Places to Visit

  • Om Beach
  • Kudle Beach
  • Half Moon Beach
  • Paradise Beach
  • Mahabaleshwar Temple

Experience Gokarna

Gokarna offers a combination of beaches, temples, coastal scenery and a peaceful travel experience.

WESTERN GHATS

Chikkamagaluru

Chikkamagaluru is a beautiful hill destination known for its coffee plantations, mountains, forests and pleasant natural surroundings.

Coffee Plantations

The region is widely associated with coffee cultivation. Visitors can experience plantation landscapes and learn about the connection between the region and coffee production.

Places to Visit

  • Mullayanagiri
  • Baba Budangiri
  • Jhari Falls
  • Hirekolale Lake
  • Coffee Estates

Adventure & Nature

Chikkamagaluru is suitable for visitors who enjoy mountain landscapes, trekking, waterfalls and peaceful natural surroundings.

KARNATAKA'S CAPITAL

Bengaluru

Bengaluru is the capital city of Karnataka and is known for its technology industry, gardens, educational institutions, cultural activities and modern urban lifestyle.

Modern Bengaluru

The city has developed into an important technology and business centre while continuing to preserve many parks, historic buildings and cultural spaces.

Places to Visit

  • Lalbagh Botanical Garden
  • Cubbon Park
  • Bengaluru Palace
  • Vidhana Soudha
  • ISKCON Temple

Culture & Lifestyle

Bengaluru offers a combination of traditional Karnataka culture and modern urban life, making it an important destination for visitors.
